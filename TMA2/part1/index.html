Welcome to your personal bookmark list!

1. Adding a Bookmark

To add a new bookmark:

  1. Enter the title of the bookmark in the "Bookmark Title" input field.
  2. Provide the URL for the bookmark in the "Bookmark URL" input field.
  3. Click the "Add Bookmark" button to save your bookmark to the list.

2. Editing a Bookmark

To edit an existing bookmark:

  1. Click the "Edit" button next to the bookmark you want to update.
  2. The title and URL fields will be pre-filled with the current bookmark's data.
  3. Modify the title or URL as needed.
  4. Click the "Save Changes" button to update the bookmark.

3. Deleting a Bookmark

To delete a bookmark:

  1. Click the "Delete" button next to the bookmark you want to remove.
  2. A confirmation prompt will appear. Click "OK" to delete the bookmark, or "Cancel" to keep it.

4. Notes on Bookmark URL

Ensure that the URL entered is valid. A valid bookmark may look like this:
  • 'youtube.com'
  • 'www.Google.ca'
  • 'https://www.google.com/maps'

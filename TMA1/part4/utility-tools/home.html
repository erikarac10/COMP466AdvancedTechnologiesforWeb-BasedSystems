Welcome to the Utility Tools App!

Use the sidebar to navigate to a utility tool that will help you out with measurements.

The Utility Tools provided are:

  • Measurement Converters:
    • Convert Weights:
      • Kilograms (kg)
      • Pounds (lb)
      • Stone (st)
    • Length Converter:
      • Meters (m)
      • Feet (ft)
      • Centimeters (cm)
      • Inches (in)
    • Area Converter:
      • Meters² (m²)
      • Feet² (ft²)
      • Centimeters² (cm²)
      • Inches² (in²)
    • Volume Converter:
      • Liters (L)
      • Gallons (gal)
      • Milliliters (mL)
      • Ounces (oz)

  • Mortgage Calculator: A simple mortgage calculator to estimate your monthly mortgage payment. Enter your loan amount, interest rate, and loan term, and you'll get the monthly payment amount.

  • Canadian Time-Zone Converter:

    Includes all time zones within Canada. Enter a date and time, and you'll get the corresponding date and time in a selected time zone.

    Time zones covered:

    • Pacific Standard Time (PST)
    • Pacific Daylight Time (PDT)
    • Mountain Standard Time (MST)
    • Mountain Daylight Time (MDT)
    • Central Standard Time (CST)
    • Central Daylight Time (CDT)
    • Eastern Standard Time (EST)
    • Eastern Daylight Time (EDT)
    • Atlantic Standard Time (AST)
    • Atlantic Daylight Time (ADT)
    • Newfoundland Standard Time (NST)
    • Newfoundland Daylight Time (NDT)
